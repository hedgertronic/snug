Hover picks the element under the pointer. Drag a loose box and pause, and its edges snap to what's under it. ⌥ keeps your box exact, ↩ copies.

One key for each of the rest

With the overlay open, press a key on whatever is highlighted or selected.

T

Copy the text

Select words anywhere on the frozen screen, even inside an image, and press ↩.

V

Record just that part

Press V again or ↩ to start, and your capture shortcut to stop. You get a .mov of just that area.

G

Capture past the fold

Scroll the app underneath and Snug stitches the frames into one tall image. ↩ when you're done.

K

Cut out the subject

Lift the subject off its background, then ↩ copies it. tab moves to the next one.

C

Copy a color

The color under the pointer, or a selection’s average, as hex. ⇧C switches to RGB, HSL or Swift.
