Privacy

    
It all happens on your Mac.

    

      
Nothing uploaded
Detection, text recognition and cutouts run on your Mac. Captures go to your clipboard, or to a file when you save.

      
Nothing running
Snug lives in the menu bar and waits for the shortcut. It records the screen only when you ask it to.

      
Two permissions
Screen Recording is required. Accessibility is recommended, so Snug can outline buttons and rows exactly instead of guessing from pixels.
